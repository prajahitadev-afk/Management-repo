1. VICE CHAIRMAN 
The Vice Chairman is the supreme authority. 
The Vice Chairman has complete control over the system. 
Capabilities include: 
 View every module  
 View every employee  
 View attendance of entire organization  
 View all bills  
 View all approvals  
 View all reports  
 View all orders  
 View transport status  
 Recruit any employee  
 Create user accounts  
 Edit any employee  
 Suspend any employee  
 Activate suspended users  
 Reset passwords  
 Delete users  
 Assign departments  
 Assign reporting hierarchy  
 View analytics  
 Download reports  
 Export data  
The Vice Chairman is the only authority that can manage Vice President accounts. 
No one can suspend or modify the Vice Chairman account. 
2. VICE PRESIDENT 
Vice Presidents have complete operational control. 
However, 
Vice Presidents CANNOT: 
 Suspend the Vice Chairman  
 Modify Vice Chairman account  
 Delete Vice Chairman  
 Change Vice Chairman permissions  
Vice Presidents can: 
 View all modules  
 Recruit employees  
 Create users  
 Suspend users  
 Approve organizational changes  
 View reports  
 Monitor attendance  
 Monitor finances  
 Monitor marketing  
 Monitor transport  
 Generate reports  
3. MANAGING DIRECTORS (MD) 
There are four Managing Directors. 
MD – Manufacturing 
Responsible for: 
 Manufacturing department  
 Factory workforce  
 Production staff  
MD – Marketing 
Responsible for: 
 Marketing Team  
 Customer Orders  
 Sales Performance  
MD – Transport 
Responsible for: 
 Logistics  
 Vehicle Operations  
 Delivery Status  
MD – Research & Development 
Responsible for: 
 Product Development  
 Laboratory Staff  
 Quality Improvement  
MD Permissions 
Each MD can: 
 View only their own department  
 Manage GMs  
 View attendance  
 View departmental reports  
 Monitor bills  
 Review approvals  
 View dashboards  
Important: 
Only Managing Directors are allowed to directly communicate with the Vice Chairman and 
Vice President through the system. 
No GM, CM, Manager, Supervisor, or Worker can directly send requests to the Vice Chairman or 
Vice President.
Communication Flow: 
Worker 
↓ 
Supervisor 
↓ 
Manager 
↓ 
Chief Manager 
↓ 
General Manager 
↓ 
Managing Director 
↓ 
Vice Chairman / Vice President 
This communication hierarchy must be strictly enforced. 
4. GENERAL MANAGER (GM) 
Each GM reports to one MD. 
Responsibilities: 
 Manage Chief Managers  
 Verify departmental operations  
 Review attendance  
 Review submitted bills  
 Approve bills  
 Reject bills  
 Return bills for correction  
 View dashboards  
 Generate reports  
IMPORTANT BILL APPROVAL RULE 
Bill approval authority starts from the General Manager level. 
Only the following positions can approve bills: 
✔ General Manager 
✔ Managing Director 
✔ Vice President
✔ Vice Chairman 
The following roles CANNOT approve bills: 
❌ Chief Manager 
❌ Manager 
❌ Supervisor 
❌ Worker 
They may only create or submit bills according to their assigned permissions. 
5. CHIEF MANAGER 
Responsibilities: 
 Manage Managers  
 View attendance  
 Review employee activities  
 Monitor department  
Cannot approve bills. 
Cannot communicate directly with VC or Vice President.
6. MANAGER 
Responsibilities: 
 Manage Supervisors  
 Verify attendance  
 Assign work  
 Monitor daily operations  
Can submit bills. 
Cannot approve bills. 
7. SUPERVISOR 
Responsibilities: 
 Manage Workers  
 Mark attendance  
 Monitor daily work  
 Submit reports  
Cannot approve bills. 
8. WORKER 
Workers can: 
 Login  
 Mark attendance  
 View attendance history  
 View assigned work  
 Submit requests  
Workers cannot: 
 Approve bills  
 View reports  
 Access higher-level information  
ATTENDANCE MANAGEMENT 
Attendance must be available for every employee. 
Features include: 
 Daily attendance  
 Present  
 Absent  
 Half Day  
 Leave  
 Weekly reports  
 Monthly reports  
 Department-wise reports  
 Search employee  
 Attendance history  
 Attendance analytics  
Managers and above can view attendance for employees under their supervision. 
VC and Vice President can view attendance across the entire organization. 
BILL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM 
The system should support complete digital bill processing. 
Bill Submission 
Employees with permission can upload: 
 Invoice Number  
 Vendor Name  
 Department  
 Amount  
 GST  
 Bill Date  
 Due Date  
 Description  
 Attach PDF/Image  
Bill Workflow 
Submitted 
↓ 
Pending Verification 
↓ 
General Manager Approval 
↓ 
Managing Director Approval (optional if required by organization policy) 
↓ 
Vice President Review (optional) 
↓ 
Vice Chairman Final Review (optional) 
↓ 
Approved 
↓ 
Passed 
↓ 
Payment Completed 
Possible bill statuses: 
 Draft  
 Submitted  
 Under Review  
 Approved  
 Passed  
 Rejected  
 Returned for Correction  
 Paid  
Every approval/rejection must capture: 
 User name  
 Role  
 Date  
 Time  
 Comments  
Maintain a complete audit trail.
